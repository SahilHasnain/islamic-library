# Index

## Persons

- **Abu Bakr al-Siddiq** (رضي الله عنه) — his status, his testimony, his role in Ghari Thawr
- **Aishah** (رضي الله عنها) — her narrations of the Prophet's ﷺ character and daily life
- **Ali ibn Abi Talib** (رضي الله عنه) — his reports, his extermination of the Khawarij
- **Fatimah** (رضي الله عنها) — her status, the Prophet's ﷺ love for her
- **Hasan and Husayn** (رضي الله عنهما) — the Prophet's ﷺ grandsons
- **Ibn Abbas** (رضي الله عنهما) — his tafsir and narrations
- **Ibn Mas'ud** (رضي الله عنه) — his reports of miracles
- **Imam Abu Hanifah** (رحمة الله عليه) — his ruling on the one who insults the Prophet
- **Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal** (رحمة الله عليه) — his position on punishment of the blasphemer
- **Imam Malik** (رحمة الله عليه) — his fatwa on the blasphemer; his reverence for hadith
- **Imam Shafi'i** (رحمة الله عليه) — his rulings on apostasy and insult
- **Jabir ibn Abdillah** (رضي الله عنهما) — his hadith of the thirty sa' of dates, the one sa' of barley
- **Jibril** (عليه السلام) — his appearance, his role, his service to the Prophet
- **Khadijah** (رضي الله عنها) — her support, her status
- **Musa** (عليه السلام) — comparison with the Prophet ﷺ, his striking of the angel of death
- **Qadi Iyad** (رحمة الله عليه) — the author; his qualifications, his methodology
- **Shatibi** (رحمة الله عليه) — quoted in ch. 4
- **Umar ibn al-Khattab** (رضي الله عنه) — his firmness, his question on Tabuk
- **Yusuf** (عليه السلام) — his beauty, his patience, comparison with the Prophet

## Prophetic Miracles

- Water flowing from fingers
- Food multiplying (Khandaq, Abu Ayyub Ansari's invitation)
- Trees speaking and moving
- The complaining palm trunk
- The poisoned goat leg speaking
- The pebbles glorifying Allah in the Prophet's ﷺ hand
- The lizard bearing witness
- Supplication for rain

## Key Theological Topics

- Excellence of the Prophet ﷺ — his lineage, his character, his physical description, his station
- Prophethood — its reality, its signs
- Intercession (*Shafa'ah*) — its proofs, its scope
- The Night Journey and Ascension (*Isra wa Mi'raj*)
- The Qur'an as miracle — its eloquence, its arrangement
- The Prophet's ﷺ knowledge of the unseen
- The Prophet's ﷺ infallibility (*'Ismah*)
- Love of the Prophet ﷺ — its obligation, its signs

## Legal Topics

- Insulting the Prophet ﷺ — forms of words that constitute insult
- Punishment of the blasphemer — capital punishment, repentance, inheritance
- Insulting Allah, His messengers, angels, books, and the Family of the Prophets
- Apostasy (*Riddah*) — rulings, repentance, inheritance
- Killing of the *zindiq* (heretic concealing disbelief)

## Qur'anic Verses Cited

Major clusters:
- Surah al-Ahzab — the Prophet's ﷺ status
- Surah al-Fath — the Prophet's ﷺ honors
- Surah al-Anfal — divine assistance in Badr
- Surah al-Ma'idah — divine protection
- Surah Yasin — the oath on the Prophet's ﷺ life
- Surah al-Qalam — the Prophet's ﷺ exalted character
